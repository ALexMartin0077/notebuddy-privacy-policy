The privacy commitment

Transparency comes first.

Read how the App handles information, what voice features may involve, and which choices remain in your hands.

01

Introduction

Welcome to NoteBuddy ("we", "our", or "the App"). NoteBuddy is a note-taking and productivity application designed to help you capture ideas, organize information, and manage your personal content.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App. Please review it to understand the features, device permissions, and external processing that may be relevant to your use.

02

Information & Local Storage

NoteBuddy is designed to store notes and other supported content locally on your device. This may include text notes, lists, tables, folders, and other content created using the App's available features.

NoteBuddy does not use Firebase or a developer-operated cloud service to store or synchronize your notes. The App's local note-storage functionality does not send your notes to us.

If you choose to attach images or files, the App may access the content you select through the device's available features and permissions.

03

Voice Recording & Voice-to-Text

NoteBuddy supports voice-related functionality, including voice recording or voice-to-text. Audio or speech input may be processed to provide the feature you choose.

Depending on the specific implementation and your device, speech processing may happen on the device or through an external speech-processing service. If an external service is used, its privacy practices and terms may also apply.

The exact processing, transmission, and retention of audio depend on the voice feature and service used by the installed version. Do not assume that all voice processing occurs offline.

04

Device Permissions

Depending on the features available in your installed version, NoteBuddy may request permissions such as:

  • Microphone: to support voice recording or speech input where required.
  • Notifications: to deliver reminders you configure.
  • Photos or files: to access content you choose to attach or import.

You can manage available permissions in your device settings. If you deny a permission, the related feature may not work as intended. Actual permissions depend on the installed version and features you use.

05

Analytics, Crash Reporting & Advertising

Based on the current developer configuration, NoteBuddy does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs.

This statement concerns SDKs included in the App. It does not rule out technical information being processed independently by your operating system, app distribution platform, or an external service used for a feature. Such services may operate under their own privacy policies.

06

Information Sharing

NoteBuddy does not upload locally stored notes to a developer-operated cloud service. We do not sell your personal notes or personal content.

When you use a feature involving an external service, such as voice-to-text, information may be processed by that service depending on the implementation. The provider's own privacy policy may apply.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or valid legal process.

07

Security & Data Retention

Notes stored on your device remain subject to the security of that device and its operating system. Use an appropriate screen lock and keep your device software updated.

Locally stored information may be permanently lost if you uninstall the App, clear its data, reset your device, or experience device failure. Recovery may not be possible if you do not have a backup.

If an external service processes information when you use a feature, that provider's retention and deletion practices may also apply.

08

Children's Privacy

NoteBuddy does not knowingly collect personal information through its local note-storage functionality. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of device permissions and external services where appropriate.

Additional children's privacy laws and Google Play requirements may apply depending on the App's intended audience, distribution, and actual functionality.

09

Your Choices & Controls

You can use the App and device settings to:

  • View, edit, and delete content using available features.
  • Choose which images or files to attach.
  • Grant or deny available device permissions.
  • Manage notifications through device settings.
  • Stop using the App or uninstall it.

Clearing App data or uninstalling NoteBuddy may permanently remove locally stored content. Back up important information where suitable backup options are available.

Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights under applicable law.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, technical implementation, or legal obligations change. The updated policy will be published on this page, with a revised effective date where appropriate.

Please revisit this page periodically to remain informed about our privacy practices.
